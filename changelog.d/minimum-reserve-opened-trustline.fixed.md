- The `minimum_reserve` criterion adds one base reserve for each trustline a call opens, so it refuses a trustline the network would reject for low reserve before signing. The trustline commands and SEP-43 signing read the trustline the call names, so removing a held trustline or changing the limit of a held credit trustline adds nothing. A trustline the account read cannot show counts as opened. In SEP-43 signing, each wallet-sourced pool share `ChangeTrust` with a nonzero limit raises the checked reserve by two base reserves, whether or not the wallet holds that trustline.
